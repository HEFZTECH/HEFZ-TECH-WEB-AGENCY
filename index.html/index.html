CASE STUDY

THE PROBLEM

Customers had to rely heavily on WhatsApp conversations to discover products and ask for prices — a slow, error-prone process with lost sales.

THE SOLUTION

HEFZ│TECH created a digital product storefront where customers can:

  1. Browse products
  2. View product information
  3. See prices
  4. Select what they want
  5. Order through WhatsApp

The result: clearer discovery, fewer repeated questions, and a direct ordering flow that moves users to WhatsApp with product context preserved.
